# Community and organization permissions

| Action | Visitor | Personal learner | Personal expert | Community reviewer | Organization member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browse active, indexed public knowledge/profiles | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Community Ask, practice, lessons, own history | Sign in | Yes | Yes | Yes | Activate personal profile |
| Private personal capture/debrief/draft editing | No | No | Own | No | Community Expert role required |
| Submit explicit public preview/excerpts | No | No | Own saved draft, two acknowledgements | No | No organization-to-public sharing |
| Community review | No | No | No | Verified email; claim; different author | Community Reviewer role required |
| Read pending community supporting excerpts | No | No | Own | Assigned reviewer only | No organization privilege applies |
| Withdraw contribution | No | No | Own | No | No organization privilege applies |
| Private organization capture/review/Ask/history | No | No community privilege | No community privilege | No community privilege | Existing organization roles and isolation apply |

Community roles are self-selected interests, not verified professional credentials.
Community reviewed means peer review of a pinned content version and selected
evidence. It is neither organization approval nor certification or guaranteed correctness.
Admin membership does not confer reviewer approval. Reviewers cannot approve their
own contributions or lessons. Worker lesson payloads omit answer keys; scoring is
server-side. Assessments are knowledge checks, not demonstrated practical competence.

Every request resolves one authenticated identity and its selected authorized scope.
Personal scopes are internal storage partitions, excluded from organization lists.
They confer no membership or role in any private organization. Public queries require
an explicit community projection, approval, active revision, matching hash and completed
indexing. Null organization IDs are never interpreted as public. Raw media URLs,
unselected excerpts, private drafts, and learning history are never public payloads.
