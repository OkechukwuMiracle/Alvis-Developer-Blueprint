# Demo recording script
Record a 45 second phone video of a stationary cardboard conveyor prop. Label it CV-07. Show two lightweight trays and a paper guide. Never demonstrate a live repair.

00 to 08 seconds: This is the CV-07 training prop. It is stopped and deenergized. This is a simulation.
08 to 20 seconds: We changed this lightweight tray, then noticed a rhythmic click near this paper guide during the simulated replay.
20 to 32 seconds: I inspected the tray guide before assuming the motor had failed.
32 to 45 seconds: I reset the paper guide. In the simulated replay, the clicking stopped.

Leave the reason and exception out of the recording deliberately.

Debrief answer about reason: The click began after the tray changed and was located near the guide. That made guide contact a useful first hypothesis, but it was not proof of a motor or bearing fault.
Debrief answer about exceptions: If no tray changed, the sound location is unknown, or there are heat, smoke or other hazards, this training case is not sufficient. Stop and ask the authorized person.
Debrief answer about outcome: The trainer confirmed the simulated click stopped after resetting the paper guide. This is an observed simulation result, not evidence of factory downtime savings.
